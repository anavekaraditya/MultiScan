## Design Context

### Users
Warehouse operators working at a device inspection station or handheld in a warehouse. Their job is to identify all 15 devices in a tray quickly, locate any unresolved device, and leave a trustworthy record for downstream operations.

### Brand Personality
Calm, precise, dependable. The interface should reduce anxiety around incorrect IMEIs and make the next action obvious during repetitive work.

### Aesthetic Direction
High-contrast light UI for bright operational environments. Use restrained indigo for navigation and focus, green only for verified matches, and amber for review/retake states. Avoid decorative dashboards, excessive cards, gradients, and ambiguous color-only status.

### Design Principles
1. Trust before speed: show why a result was accepted or held for review.
2. One clear next action: the primary button always advances the operator’s current job.
3. Cell-level recovery: one difficult device must not force a full-tray rescan.
4. Visible progress: show 15 expected positions, accepted count, unresolved count, and sync state.
5. Bright-room legibility: large touch targets, strong contrast, short labels, and no color-only meaning.
